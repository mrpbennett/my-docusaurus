# Lessons

## Don't guess who wrote a post from its formatting

I called `mitmproxy.md` and `kulalanvim.md` AI-sounding because of emoji headings and stock section names. Paul wrote every 2024 and 2025 post by hand.

- **Rule:** Never label content as machine-written from style alone. Ask the author, or describe the pattern without attributing it.

## Read grep output before citing it as evidence

I cited two posts as having in-page anchor links. The hits came from a different grep in the same command (admonition counts).

- **Rule:** Run one check per command when the result will be quoted as evidence, and confirm the match before stating it.
