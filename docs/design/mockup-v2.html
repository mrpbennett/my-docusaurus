No. 028·Developer / Terminal

Terminal setup, 2026

A year on Omarchy: what stayed, what went, and the handful of scripts that make the whole thing feel like one tool.

Paul Bennett
Lead Solutions Engineer | 08 Oct 2026 · 9 min read AI-assisted

Every year I write down how my terminal is set up, mostly so future me can work out why something is the way it is. This year the theme is subtraction: fewer plugins, fewer configs, and more defaults that I've decided are good enough.

The short version: Ghostty, Zellij, Helix and Yazi, glued together with a couple of hundred lines of shell.

The shell

I'm still on zsh, but the plugin manager is gone. Starship handles the prompt, atuin handles history, and that's it.

~/.zshrccopy
# history that syncs across machines
eval "$(atuin init zsh)"
eval "$(starship init zsh)"
export EDITOR=hx
The best config is the one you can rebuild from memory on a fresh machine.

By the numbers

212lines of dotfiles, down from 900
4tools I open every day
0zsh plugins
NoteAll of this lives in a public dotfiles repo, managed with mise and stow.

Multiplexer

Zellij won, for the reasons I covered in Zellij vs tmux. Floating panes for one-off commands sold me on it.
